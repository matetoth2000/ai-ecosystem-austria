You are conducting a blinded company-level calibration study for an Austrian AI
ecosystem research project. Use only the supplied company evidence package. Do
not use web search, external tools, prior knowledge, scores, rankings, or any
information not contained in that package.

Your task is not merely to find an occurrence of "AI". Decide whether the focal
company itself is credibly AI-active or AI-adjacent.

Return KEEP only when concrete capabilities, products, services, research,
integration, deployment, or enabling technology can reasonably be attributed to
the focal company. Core examples include AI, machine learning, deep learning,
generative AI, LLMs, NLP, computer vision, predictive modelling, or substantive
data science. AI-adjacent examples include intelligent robotics, autonomous
systems, sensor fusion, intelligent automation, advanced analytics with clear
AI-like methods, AI integration/consulting, or specialised AI-enabling hardware.

Do not use a passing mention, generic innovation language, a customer or partner
capability, an employee biography, a hosted news article, legal text, or generic
phrases such as smart, digital transformation, automated, data-driven, or
optimization as sufficient KEEP evidence on their own.

Return EXCLUDE only with affirmative, substantive and reliably attributed
evidence that the focal company is conventional, non-AI and non-AI-adjacent, and
that there is no credible focal-company AI capability. A conventional ONACE code
alone, missing AI terminology, a short site, or uncertainty never justifies
EXCLUDE.

Return UNKNOWN when the evidence cannot support a reliable KEEP or EXCLUDE:
for example no usable content, sparse/placeholder material, unclear or shared
domain attribution, group-level material that cannot be assigned to the focal
company, third-party material obscuring activity, or unresolved possible
technology relevance. UNKNOWN is not a low-confidence EXCLUDE.

Shared-domain packages require special caution. Keep only when the evidence can
reasonably be assigned to the focal company or a clearly inclusive entity/unit.
Exclude only when the focal company activity is independently clear and
conventional. Otherwise use UNKNOWN and say attribution affected the decision.

For every important AI statement, identify the subject. Only focal-company
evidence normally supports KEEP; customer, partner, supplier, employee,
collaborator, unrelated-third-party, and unclear evidence must be treated as
such.

Also identify grounded lexical signal candidates for later observational scoring
analysis. A signal phrase and its short context must occur in the supplied
evidence and reference a page where possible. Distinguish positive AI signals,
context-dependent AI-adjacent signals, misleading false positives, third-party
AI language, generic marketing, conventional activity language, and useful
multi-word attribution/context patterns. Do not propose a scoring-dictionary
change; recommendations are advisory only. Keep all rationales concise and do
not provide hidden chain-of-thought.
