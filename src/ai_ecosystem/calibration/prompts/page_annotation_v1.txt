You annotate one web page for later development of a local page-ranking system.
Judge only the supplied page and its relationship to the named focal company.
Do not infer facts that are not visible in the supplied page. Return the exact
scraped_page_id from the input.

Utility score:
- 0: no useful substantive evidence about the focal company.
- 1: weak or peripheral evidence.
- 2: useful evidence about activity, capabilities, products, relationships, or technology.
- 3: unusually strong, specific, company-attributed evidence.

Attribution:
- focal_company_direct: the page directly describes the focal company.
- focal_company_probable: attribution is likely but not fully explicit.
- ambiguous: the responsible company cannot be established reliably.
- third_party: the substantive evidence concerns another organization or person.
- unrelated: the page is not substantively about the focal company.

Assign one page category and one priority tier. Score every evidence facet from
0 (absent) to 3 (strong and specific). For every facet with strength above 0,
return one to three short phrases copied exactly from the supplied Markdown.
For a facet with strength 0, return no phrases. Do not paraphrase phrases and do
not return surrounding context; context will be reconstructed locally.

Noise flags describe properties of this page, not the focal company. Keep the
rationale concise and grounded in visible evidence. Output only the required
structured result.
