ASSESSMENTS AND ATTRIBUTION
ai_active: develops/trains AI, offers its own AI products, integrates AI, substantively researches/tests AI, or uses AI internally. Technical evidence may establish AI without the literal term. Ordinary automation, statistics, sensors, digitization and marketing buzzwords alone do not.
ai_adjacent: offers AI-enabling infrastructure, tooling, data or support. Explicit AI offerings qualify. Without explicit AI connection, infer only from GPU/HPC compute offered to others or specialized annotation/labeled datasets clearly suitable for model training/evaluation; state the inference and cite the offering. Generic hosting, data ownership, publishing and consulting do not qualify by themselves. Pure retail/resale of AI consumer products, investment and membership alone establish neither assessment. Both assessments may be true.
Assessment value true means qualifying activity is established; false means not established in sufficiently informative supplied evidence, NOT never active; null means evidence is insufficient or ambiguous. Current offerings and active research/pilots qualify. Plans and clearly ended activity do not; briefly note them in the reason. Treat undated present-tense offerings as current unless contradicted. Decisive uncertainty about currentness means null.
Attribution.value asks whether the supplied evidence identifies/describes the focal business sufficiently to assess it, regardless of whether AI is found. On the ORBIS-linked domain, use the business presented by homepage/about pages as the default subject. A trading name or different legal name alone does not invalidate this website-business attribution or prove legal equivalence; record unresolved identity concerns in the existing reason. A redirect alone proves neither identity nor nonidentity. Contradictory evidence that the domain belongs to an unrelated business warrants uncertainty.
If the focal record is explicitly a DISTINCT smaller business within the website's institution or group, assess that business's own activity. Hospital AI does not transfer to its separately identified parking company. If the focal record is a supported REGIONAL MEMBER of the same group, a general group-wide AI statement may qualify under this study definition even without demonstrated regional use; activity explicitly limited to another region does not. Regional membership without qualifying group AI evidence is not positive. Never treat a customer's, supplier's or unrelated organization's activity as focal activity. First-person company/product/service claims can qualify for the identified website business; news or customer stories qualify only when they explicitly describe that business's contribution. Domain membership, logos, job requirements and aspirations alone do not prove AI activity.
Set attribution_scope separately for EACH assessment: website_business for the domain's default business; focal_unit for a distinctly identified smaller focal business; group for supported regional membership plus general group-wide activity; null whenever that assessment is false or null. A positive assessment needs evidence_ids supporting both the qualifying activity and its scope. For group scope cite the membership and group AI statement; say in the reason that regional use is not directly demonstrated. A different-region-only claim is not group-wide evidence. Assessment basis describes only a POSITIVE focal decision: explicit for ai_active; explicit, inferred_compute or inferred_data for ai_adjacent. For false/null use basis=none, even if referenced evidence or the short reason documents another organization's explicit AI.
Evidence adequacy concerns whether supplied material permits assessment, not whether the live site was fully crawled. A clear qualifying statement can suffice; missing text does not prove absence. Attribution uncertainty is separate from content adequacy.
